About Olavi Beauty

Olavi Beauty supplies beauty essentials to the businesses that sell them. Our range covers cotton swabs, cotton pads and pure cotton balls, lip gloss and lipstick, and LED makeup mirrors — the fast‑moving items beauty brands, retailers, distributors and online sellers reorder most.

We keep the catalogue focused rather than broad. Each line is chosen for two things: consistent quality at a wholesale price that leaves room for resale, and enough flexibility to carry your brand. Most items ship in bulk, and many are available for OEM, ODM and private label projects — from logo and color to retail packaging.

Our role is straightforward. You tell us your market, target price point and packaging requirements; we come back with specifications, samples and a quotation you can work from.

If you are building or expanding a beauty range, we would be glad to quote it.
